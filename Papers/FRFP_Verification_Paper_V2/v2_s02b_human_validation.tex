\section{Human Validation Under FRFP}
\label{sec:human_validation}

Lean provides a correctness oracle for formal derivations. FRFP therefore does
\emph{not} assign humans the task of re-checking proof steps that the Lean
kernel has already validated. Instead, FRFP assigns humans a different role:
governing claim intent, premise legitimacy, and publication scope.

This project treats machine-checked proof and human governance as complementary,
not interchangeable.

\subsection{Why Human Validation Is Still Required}

A kernel-accepted proof guarantees internal logical correctness relative to
stated axioms and definitions. It does not guarantee that:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item the formal statement matches the intended policy or scientific claim,
  \item the retained premises are normatively acceptable,
  \item the selected abstraction boundary is appropriate for deployment.
\end{enumerate}

Under FRFP, these are tacit-space judgments and remain human responsibilities.

\subsection{FRFP-Compliant Validation Protocol}

Human validation follows the same FRFP separation principles used in the rest of
this paper:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Statement validation}: confirm each theorem statement is semantically
    aligned with the corresponding theory claim before publication claims
    are finalized.
  \item \textbf{Axiom legitimacy review}: verify each retained axiom is either an
        explicit FRFP premise or source-grounded in published references.
  \item \textbf{Normative residue review}: for each primitive assumption, record what
        is mathematical skeleton and what is normative residue.
  \item \textbf{Boundary authorization}: approve publication-level claims only after
        human review of theorem mappings, caveats, and scope limits.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Validation Artifacts}

To make human validation auditable, we maintain four artifact classes:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Theory-to-Lean correspondence tables (Appendix~A and Appendix~F).
  \item Axiom inventory and status audit (Appendix~A).
  \item Architecture/dependency traceability (Appendix~B).
  \item Proposal log for theory refinements surfaced by formalization (Appendix~C).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Operational Rule}

In this FRFP formalization, \textbf{kernel acceptance is necessary but not
sufficient} for publication claims. A claim is publication-valid only when both
conditions hold:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Lean accepts the formal proof (proof correctness criterion).
  \item Human governance accepts semantic alignment, premise legitimacy, and
        normative scope (publication criterion).
\end{enumerate}

This dual criterion preserves FRFP's human-grounded governance commitments while
retaining the rigor of machine-checked proof.

\subsection{Compact Principle}

\textbf{Lean verifies proofs; humans validate commitments.}
