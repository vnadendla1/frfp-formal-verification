\section{Related Work}

Lean-based formalization papers provide established publication patterns for
reporting mechanized mathematics: theorem statement scope, formalization deltas,
engineering constraints, and artifact links.

\subsection{Lean Formalization Literature}

Representative projects include formalizations in algebraic number theory,
topology, and combinatorics, as cataloged by the Lean community papers index.
Examples include cap-set formalization, perfectoid spaces, and condensed
mathematics lines of work.

\subsection{FLT Regular-Primes Reference}

The formalization of Fermat's Last Theorem for regular primes in Lean provides a
direct methodological benchmark for communicating proof strategy and
formalization challenges:
\begin{itemize}
	\item ITP 2023 short paper: \emph{Fermat's Last Theorem for Regular Primes}~\cite{Dokchitser2023FLT}.
	\item AFM 2025 expanded paper: \emph{A complete formalization of Fermat's Last Theorem for regular primes in Lean}~\cite{vanDoorn2025FLT}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Positioning of FRFP}

FRFP differs by emphasizing governance and protocol semantics while following
established formalization reporting standards for theorem traceability and
reproducibility. In particular, FRFP contributes a combined audit pattern:
axiom minimization, theory-to-Lean theorem correspondence, and executable
reproducibility in one publication package.
