\section{Conclusion}

This verification companion presents a machine-checked account of FRFP with
explicit traceability from theory claims to Lean theorems, documented basis
reduction, and reproducibility artifacts.

The resulting publication package combines:
\begin{enumerate}
	\item A complete Lean formalization record with audited assumptions.
	\item A reduced and justified axiom basis.
	\item Novel theorem outcomes generated by formal proof obligations.
	\item A reproducible build and artifact-evaluation workflow.
\end{enumerate}

Together these provide an inspectable, executable, and independently auditable
verification reference for FRFP.
