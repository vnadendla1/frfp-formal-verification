\section{Discussion and Future Work}

\subsection{Current Limits}

Some retained assumptions are policy- or interpretation-facing and therefore are
not expected to be eliminated purely through formal derivation. Their role is
made explicit to separate normative commitments from mathematical structure.

The formalization is also bounded by model scope: machine-checked correctness is
guaranteed relative to the declared definitions and assumptions, not relative to
all possible real-world deployment details.

\subsection{Extension Targets}

Future work includes extending theorem coverage for edge-case behaviors,
strengthening correspondence metadata, and expanding executable case studies.
Priority items include deeper stochastic governance results, richer trajectory
analysis, and stronger automation around theorem-to-paper synchronization.

\subsection{Maintenance Plan}

Planned updates include periodic compatibility checks against Lean and mathlib
releases while preserving reproducibility for archived versions. The pinned
artifact remains the reference build for publication claims.
