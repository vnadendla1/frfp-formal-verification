\subsection{Robustness Checks}

Results were robust to:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Exclusion of R1-only selections,
    \item Bootstrap resampling across cases,
    \item Removal of cases with lowest inter-rater agreement.
\end{itemize}

No evidence was found that protocol structuring degraded performance in mechanically crisp cases.

\subsection{Summary of Empirical Findings}

Across 25 cases and 10 raters:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Protocol structuring improved alignment with independent reports in governance-heavy domains.
    \item Differences were smaller in mechanistic single-cause failures.
    \item Protocol structuring reduced dispersion and unsupported-claim flags.
\end{itemize}

These results are consistent with the conditional hypothesis and indicate measurable alignment gains in governance-heavy cases. The broader question of universal protocol adoption is treated as an operational consideration discussed in Section 6.6.
