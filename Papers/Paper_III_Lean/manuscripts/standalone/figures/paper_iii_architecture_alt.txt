Figure 1 separates representation and adequacy, nominal and valid authority, and possible criterion revision. Nominal charter membership and CanBear jointly define a valid root. A valid-root decision can pass through permitted delegation to an actual authorization or closure occurrence with valid standing; a Machine may execute that decision without becoming a valid root. Optional principalhood requires a singleton nominal root set, separately from competence. Completion eliminates realized governance into an actual occurrence and does not prove liveness. Origin or GovInd plus realization supplies nominal membership only through an adopted rule. Discovery, proposal, admission, criterion change and representation revision are possible transitions, not automatic implications.
